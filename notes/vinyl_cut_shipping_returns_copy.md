## Shipping

We ship flat-rate across the Mountain West and Pacific Coast — California, Oregon, Washington, Nevada, Arizona, Colorado, Utah, and New Mexico. We don't ship outside this region, by design: shorter transit times mean less time for a record to get knocked around, and it keeps us close enough to actually stand behind every package.

**Delivery estimates:** most orders arrive within 3–5 business days of shipping. You'll get a shipping confirmation the moment your order's packed.

**Packing:** every record ships in a rigid mailer, padded corners, no exceptions — new pressing or used find, doesn't matter.

---

## Returns

**Used records are sold as-graded.** We inspect and grade every used record by hand before it ever hits the shelf (see our [Grading Guide](#)), so what's listed is what you're getting — no surprises, no returns for condition once it's left our hands.

**Damaged in transit is a different story.** If something arrives cracked, warped, or otherwise worse than what we shipped, tell us within 7 days and we'll make it right — replacement if we've got another copy, refund if we don't.

**New pressings** can be returned unopened within 14 days for a full refund. Once the shrink wrap's off, it's yours — same as any record shop.
