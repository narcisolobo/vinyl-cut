## FAQ

**Is this a real store?**
No — The Vinyl Cut is a fictional portfolio demo. No real orders are placed or fulfilled.

**Do you buy records?**
Yes — see [Sell Your Records](#) for how it works.

**Where do you ship?**
Flat-rate, across the Mountain West and Pacific Coast. Full details on [Shipping & Returns](#).

**How do you grade used vinyl?**
By hand, against the same scale every time — see our [Grading Guide](#).

**Can I return something?**
Depends what you bought — used records are sold as-graded, new pressings can go back unopened. Full policy on [Shipping & Returns](#).

**Can I listen to a record before I buy it?**
Bring it to the counter and we'll point you to the listening station right next door. Headphones are already plugged in.

**Can you hold a record for me?**
Yes, for 48 hours — just ask at the counter. After that, it's back in the crates for the next person to find.

**Do you participate in Record Store Day?**
Every single one since it began in 2008. Check our socials closer to the date for our RSD lineup and any doorbuster drops.

**Do you sell turntables, sleeves, or other accessories?**
Not currently — we're all about the records themselves. For gear and accessories, your local audio shop's got you covered.
